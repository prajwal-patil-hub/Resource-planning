Why there is no predicted date

Two items of the same kind can differ by a week for reasons nothing in this system can see — the client goes quiet, the fix turns out to sit under something else, someone more urgent arrives. A single date hides that spread behind a number that looks certain.

So the product answers with the spread itself. "85% of work like this finished within four working days" can be planned against and can be checked afterwards. "Thursday" can only be believed, and then broken.

Where fewer than {{ min_sample }} comparable items have finished, nothing is offered at all. A range drawn from three items is an anecdote wearing a statistic's clothes, and the first time it is wrong it takes every other figure here down with it.
