How to read this without being wrong

Share is a share of finished items, not of the team's time or money. Ten trivial items and ten hard ones both read as ten.

Time alive sums how long each item existed in working hours. It is a measure of duration and commitment, not of labour: an item can sit open for a fortnight while consuming an afternoon.

Waiting on them is the most defensible number here, because it comes from a state somebody explicitly set. If it looks wrong for a client, the fix is to record waiting on client when it happens — not to adjust the report.

There is no cost or revenue anywhere on this page. The product has no access to either, and inventing a rate to multiply by would turn a set of honest measurements into a made-up number that reads as an accounting fact.
